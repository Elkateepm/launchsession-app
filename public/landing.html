Why LaunchSession

One evening,
two ways to run it.

Same club, same night, same people. Flip the switch to see what changes when everything lives in one place.

  1. 17:45Before doors open
    Clipboard, folder, group chat

    Print the register, dig out the medical folder, then scroll the group chat to see who is actually covering tonight.

    Your phone

    Tonight's register, medical flags and team are already on the phone of everyone on shift.

  2. 18:00Doors open
    Clipboard

    A queue at the clipboard. One person ticks names while another tries to keep a headcount.

    Live register

    Tap each name as they arrive. The headcount updates on every phone in the room.

  3. 18:40Someone gets hurt
    Accident form

    Find a blank accident form, fill it in later from memory and hope it reaches the right person.

    Accident book

    Log it on the spot with a body map, the first aid given and whether a parent was told.

  4. 19:15A worry about a child
    Email

    A note in a diary, or an email to your safeguarding lead that sits in an inbox with everything else.

    Safeguarding

    Raise a concern from your phone. It goes straight into the safeguarding record, with an owner and an audit trail.

  5. 20:00Locking up
    Headcount

    Count the clipboard twice and check the toilets. Is anyone still here?

    Close checklist

    The session will not close while anyone is still signed in.

  6. MondayThe funder wants numbers
    Spreadsheet

    Retype a term of paper registers into a spreadsheet the night before the deadline.

    Reports

    Attendance and impact figures are already counted, ready to export.
